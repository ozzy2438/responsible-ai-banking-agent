# Escalation Matrix

| Trigger | Risk | Route | Autonomous final decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| General approved FAQ | LOW | None | Bounded explanation only |
| Authorised account or transaction explanation | MEDIUM | Customer service if evidence is incomplete | No assumption |
| Complaint | MEDIUM | Complaints | No final complaint outcome |
| Credit, lending, limit, eligibility | HIGH | Lending specialist | Never |
| Fraud or suspicious activity | HIGH | Fraud operations or legal/compliance | Never |
| Financial hardship | HIGH | Hardship | Never |
| Personalised advice or investment request | HIGH | Legal/compliance | Never |
| Large or unusual transaction | HIGH | Fraud operations | Never |
| Identity conflict, policy exception, legal issue | HIGH | Legal/compliance | Never |
| Unknown or conflicting evidence | HIGH | Customer service or legal/compliance | Never |

Handoffs include the redacted request, verified facts, missing information,
control reason, actions already taken, and recommended controlled next step.
