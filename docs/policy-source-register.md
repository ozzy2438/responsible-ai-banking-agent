# Policy Source Register

V1 policy content is synthetic, versioned, and committed with a manifest hash.
It must never be presented as the policy, rates, fees, eligibility, or terms of a
real financial institution.

| Source ID | Purpose | Authority | Update rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| `POL-GENERAL-001` | General process explanations | Synthetic approved fixture | Version and re-hash |
| `POL-SECURITY-001` | Credential and card-data safety | Synthetic approved fixture | Security review |
| `POL-ESCALATE-001` | Escalation routing | Approved V1 control matrix | Governance review |

Every factual customer answer must cite one of these sources or a specific,
authorised synthetic account/transaction record. Missing provenance fails closed.
