A banking assistant that knows what it isn’t allowed to do

This reference implementation answers verified banking questions from synthetic data, then deterministically routes anything regulated, high-impact, uncertain, or unsupported to a human reviewer — every time, by design, not by prompting. Pick a synthetic persona below to try it.

What this demonstrates

  • Deterministic risk boundary — LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH classification runs in plain code, not a prompt; a model can improve wording but cannot lower risk, widen access, or change the outcome.
  • Authorization & isolation — every account lookup is re-verified against the signed-in actor; cross-customer access fails closed.
  • Privacy redaction — submitted passwords, PINs, and card numbers are stripped before storage or reasoning, and the request still escalates.
  • Human escalation — credit, fraud, hardship, legal, and unclear requests always route to a reviewer queue with acknowledge / route / close actions.
  • Cited, evidenced answers — every factual claim carries a source citation; unsupported claims fail closed instead of guessing.
